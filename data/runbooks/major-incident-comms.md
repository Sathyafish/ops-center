## Status page updates

- **SEV1:** Update every **30 minutes** minimum until resolved.
- **SEV2:** Update every **60 minutes** or on material change.

## Director escalation triggers

Engage L3 / Director when:

- Customer impact exceeds **10%** of transactions for **15+ minutes**, or
- Any **Tier-0** app exceeds **30 minute** SLA breach, or
- Public media / executive inquiry received.

## Bridge roles

- **Incident Commander** — decision authority
- **Comms Lead** — status page and internal slack
- **Scribe** — timeline in OPS ticket

## Message structure

Impact → Scope (regions/apps) → Workaround if any → Next update time (UTC).
